How it works

  1. Questions for this job. From the advert, your kind of work, and the questions every interviewer asks.
  2. Answer your way. Type it, or press Answer out loud and talk – it writes down what you say. It can read the question out too.
  3. Get marked. A score out of 10 on what interviewers listen for: a real example, the STAR shape (Situation, Task, Action, Result), what you did, a result, the right length.
  4. Improve. Specific tips, and examples from your own work history you could use. Try again and watch your score go up.
