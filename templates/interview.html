Practise for my kind of work

How it works

  1. Questions for this job – from what the advert asks for, your kind of work, and the questions almost every interview has.
  2. Answer your way – type, or press Answer out loud and speak (your browser turns it into text; nothing is recorded).
  3. Instant feedback – a score out of 10, the STAR check for “tell me about a time…” questions, what went well and what to improve.
  4. Use your own examples – it points to things from My career that would fit.
  5. Try again until you're happy – every attempt is saved so you can see yourself improve.
