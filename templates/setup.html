Your two browser buttons

Drag both buttons onto your browser's bookmarks bar (press Ctrl+Shift+B if you can't see the bar):

Send to Job Hunt HQ Fill this application
  1. On a job advert (LinkedIn, Indeed…), click Send to Job Hunt HQ. The app scores it and writes your cover letter.
  2. Click the site's Apply button to reach the employer's form.
  3. On the form, click Fill this application. It fills your details, the message and cover letter boxes, attaches your CV and the cover letter as a PDF, and highlights everything it filled.
  4. Check the highlighted fields, answer anything left blank, press the site's Submit, then press I've submitted – mark as applied in the blue box.

The app must be running (run.bat). Both buttons open a small Job Hunt HQ window for a moment – if your browser blocks it, allow pop-ups for that site (LinkedIn, Indeed, and the first time on each employer site). The Fill button contains a private key, so other websites can't read your details. If you ever share your bookmarks, delete data\access_token.txt and drag the buttons again.
