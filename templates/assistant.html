Your job-application assistant

A small helper that floats on every job site – Indeed, LinkedIn, Reed, NHS Jobs, Workday and the rest. On an advert it scores the job against your details. On an application form it fills in what it can, suggests answers from your own work history and examples, and attaches your CV and letter. It never presses Submit – you always do that.

Install it (2 minutes, once)

  1. Download the assistant

    It's already connected to your account – don't share the file.

  2. Unzip it: right-click the download → Extract All. Keep the folder somewhere safe, like Documents.
  3. Open your browser's extensions page – type this in the address bar:
    chrome://extensions

    Opera GX: opera://extensions · Edge: edge://extensions

  4. Switch on Developer mode (top right).
  5. Click Load unpacked and choose the Job Hunt HQ Assistant folder.
  6. Pin it: click the jigsaw icon → pin Job Hunt HQ Assistant.

Using it

  • On a job advert – the round Job Hunt HQ button shows your fit score. Click it to see why, and Save it and write my cover letter.
  • On an application form – Fill this page for me fills your details and attaches your CV and letter. Suggest answers drafts each question from your own experience; edit it, then Put it in the form.
  • Ask your assistant – e.g. “Which of my experiences best fits this role?”{% if not ai %} (needs AI switched on by the owner){% endif %}
  • After you submit – Mark as applied, and it's tracked in Applications.

It only appears on job boards, employers' careers pages and application forms – never on sites like YouTube, Google or social media, and it sends nothing to Job Hunt HQ from other sites. Showing up somewhere it shouldn't? Open it and choose Don't show on this site. You can still open it anywhere with its button in your browser's toolbar.

Drag the button anywhere on the screen. Press – at the top of the panel to minimise it to a small dot – it stays small on every page until you click it again. Questions about you personally (health, criminal record, diversity) are always left for you to answer. Answers only use what's in your own details – check each one before you submit.
