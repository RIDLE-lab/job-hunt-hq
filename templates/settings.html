Gmail

Connect Gmail and Job Hunt HQ reads replies from employers – acknowledgements, interviews, rejections, offers – and keeps your Applications up to date. It only reads; it never sends or deletes anything. You need a Gmail app password: go to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords (2-Step Verification must be on), create one called “Job Hunt HQ”, and paste the 16 letters here.

Your emails stay private. Nobody reads your Gmail through Job Hunt HQ – not the owner and not anyone else using it. It's read automatically, only to spot emails about your job applications; for those it keeps the sender, subject, date and a short preview so your Applications page stays up to date. All other emails are ignored and nothing is saved from them. It can't send, change or delete anything in your Gmail.

You can disconnect any time by removing the app password here (or deleting it in your Google account), download everything that's stored with Download my data, and ask how your data is used, have it corrected or deleted under UK GDPR – see Privacy and your data.
