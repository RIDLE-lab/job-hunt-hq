Your email

Connect the email account employers write to, and Job Hunt HQ reads their replies – acknowledgements, interviews, rejections, offers – and keeps your Applications up to date. It only reads; it never sends or deletes anything. Gmail, Yahoo, iCloud, AOL, Zoho, GMX, Fastmail and most other services work with an app password you make in your email account’s security settings.

Your emails stay private. Nobody reads your email through Job Hunt HQ – not the owner and not anyone else using it. It's read automatically, only to spot emails about your job applications; for those it keeps the sender, subject, date and a short preview so your Applications page stays up to date. All other emails are ignored and nothing is saved from them. It can't send, change or delete anything in your email.

You can disconnect any time by removing the app password here (or deleting it in your email account), download everything that's stored with Download my data, and ask how your data is used, have it corrected or deleted under UK GDPR – see Privacy and your data.

Make one at your account’s app passwords page, call it “Job Hunt HQ”, and paste it here.
