How it works

Five steps, from knowing what you want to starting your new job.

  1. 1

    Tell it what you want

    Choose the kinds of job you’re after, where you live and how far you’ll travel. Add your CV and it learns your experience.

  2. 2

    Jobs come to you

    It searches the job sites for you and puts the jobs that suit you most at the top, with the reasons why.

  3. 3

    Apply with less effort

    Your cover letter is already written. The assistant fills in the application form. You check it and press Submit.

  4. 4

    Walk in prepared

    Replies are tracked for you, so nothing is missed. Then practise the questions for that job and get tips to improve.

  5. 5

    Get the job

    Nobody can promise an offer. But you’ll apply to the right jobs, never miss a reply and walk in prepared – the best chance you can give yourself.
